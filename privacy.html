Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Overview

Poppel is designed to be enjoyed without creating an account. We do not sell your personal information.

Game data

Poppel stores game progress, settings, high scores, and achievements locally on your device. This data is used to provide game features and is not sent to us by the current version of the app.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled in a future version of Poppel, ads may be provided by Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location, app interaction data, and other information in accordance with its own privacy policy to deliver and measure ads. We will update this policy and App Store privacy disclosures before releasing advertising.

In-app purchases

Poppel may offer a one-time Ad-Free purchase through Apple’s App Store. Purchase processing is handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment-card information.

Children

Poppel is not directed to children under 13. If advertising is introduced, we will configure it and the app’s disclosures for the app’s applicable age rating.

Changes

We may update this policy as Poppel evolves. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact MonarchDevelopmentSupport@gmail.com.
